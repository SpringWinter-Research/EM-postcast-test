1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

1.1 Project Overview

Emerging Talks Podcast Matcher is a digital platform designed to connect podcast hosts with relevant guests through intelligent discovery, profile-based recommendations, communication and interview-booking workflows.

The platform will provide a centralised environment where podcast hosts can discover credible experts, founders, entrepreneurs, authors, professionals and content creators for their shows.

Similarly, individuals seeking podcast appearances will be able to discover relevant podcasts, evaluate opportunities, approach hosts and manage their interview applications.

The platform aims to reduce the time, effort and uncertainty involved in finding suitable podcast collaborations.

Unlike a conventional directory, Emerging Talks Podcast Matcher will facilitate the complete collaboration journey:

Discovery → Matching → Connection → Pitching → Acceptance → Scheduling → Recording → Publishing → Reviews

The long-term objective is to establish Emerging Talks as a professional podcast collaboration network.

1.2 Business Problem

The podcast ecosystem has two interconnected challenges.

For podcast hosts:

- Difficulty identifying relevant and credible guests.
- Time-consuming manual guest research.
- Receiving irrelevant or generic pitches.
- Lack of a centralised guest discovery platform.
- Difficulty managing guest enquiries and interview schedules.
- Limited access to experts outside their existing networks.

For podcast guests:

- Difficulty identifying podcasts that accept guests.
- Limited access to podcast hosts.
- Lack of clarity about audience relevance and show quality.
- Time-consuming cold outreach.
- Difficulty presenting professional credentials and interview experience.
- No centralised system to track podcast applications and appearances.

1.3 Proposed Solution

Develop a two-sided marketplace that provides:

1. Podcast host registration and profiles.
2. Podcast guest registration and professional profiles.
3. Searchable podcast directory.
4. Searchable guest directory.
5. Intelligent matching recommendations.
6. Personalised discovery.
7. Interview invitations and guest applications.
8. Internal messaging.
9. Media kit management.
10. Interview scheduling.
11. Booking management.
12. Episode and publication tracking.
13. Reviews and reputation management.
14. Subscription and premium functionality.
15. Platform administration and moderation.

12. BUSINESS RULES

The following rules shall govern the platform.

BR-001: A user may have both host and guest roles.

BR-002: A host may register multiple podcasts.

BR-003: A guest may maintain multiple areas of expertise.

BR-004: A guest application must be associated with a specific podcast.

BR-005: An invitation must be associated with a host and a guest.

BR-006: A booking can only be created from an eligible accepted invitation or application, or an explicitly permitted direct booking workflow.

BR-007: A booking must have a valid status.

BR-008: A completed collaboration may be reviewed by eligible participants.

BR-009: A user cannot review the same collaboration repeatedly.

BR-010: Match scores must be calculated using defined compatibility rules.

BR-011: Paid features must only be accessible to users with valid entitlements.

BR-012: Expired subscriptions must trigger the configured access restrictions.

BR-013: A user must not access private information belonging to another user without permission.

BR-014: Blocked users must not be recommended to one another.

BR-015: Administrators must have an auditable record of sensitive actions.

BR-016: User-declared audience figures must not be represented as independently verified statistics.

BR-017: Podcast listings must be subject to moderation and accuracy policies.

BR-018: Users must be able to report fraudulent or inappropriate behaviour.

14. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

The platform shall not be considered ready for commercial release until the following conditions are met.

Registration and Accounts

- A new user can register successfully.
- Email verification works.
- Password recovery works.
- Users can log in and out.
- Role selection works.
- Users can maintain multiple roles.

Profiles

- Hosts can create and edit podcast profiles.
- Guests can create and edit professional profiles.
- Media uploads work.
- Profile information persists after refresh.
- Profile visibility controls work.

Discovery

- Podcast search returns relevant results.
- Guest search returns relevant results.
- Filters work individually and in combination.
- Sorting produces correct results.
- Saved profiles persist.

Matching

- Match scores are generated correctly.
- Scores respond to profile changes.
- Compatibility explanations are displayed.
- Incompatible profiles are not incorrectly recommended.
- New users are not unfairly penalised for missing audience data.

Applications and Messaging

- Guests can submit applications.
- Hosts can review applications.
- Hosts can send invitations.
- Users receive status notifications.
- Messages persist across sessions.
- Unread counts work correctly.
- Spam and abuse controls are active.

Booking

- Accepted applications can progress to booking.
- Booking dates and time zones are handled correctly.
- Confirmation emails work.
- Rescheduling and cancellation work.
- Booking records persist.

Payments

- The selected PSP checkout works.
- Payment events from the selected PSP are verified server-side.
- Valid payments activate the correct entitlements.
- Failed payments do not grant paid access.
- Refunds and cancellations update entitlements correctly.
- Duplicate webhook events do not duplicate transactions or access.

Administration

- Administrators can manage users.
- Administrators can moderate listings.
- Administrators can review reports.
- Administrative actions are logged.

Security and Reliability

- Unauthorised users cannot access protected data.
- User permissions are enforced server-side.
- Backups are configured.
- Error logging is functional.
- The application works on desktop and mobile.

21. FINAL PRODUCT DEFINITION

Emerging Talks Podcast Matcher shall be developed as a professional podcast collaboration marketplace connecting podcast hosts with relevant experts, entrepreneurs, authors, founders, creators and professionals.

The platform shall provide the infrastructure required to discover, evaluate, connect, communicate, schedule and track podcast collaborations.

Its core differentiation shall be the combination of:

- Professional guest and podcast profiles.
- Intelligent compatibility-based matching.
- Structured interview application workflows.
- Integrated communication.
- Interview booking and tracking.
- Reputation and verification.
- A scalable professional podcast network.

The product must not be limited to a contact directory or a matching interface.

Its commercial and strategic value will come from successfully converting discovery into real podcast collaborations.

The ultimate objective is to make Emerging Talks a trusted destination for finding the right podcast, the right guest and the right conversation.

## 21. PRODUCT DECISIONS, MVP DEFINITION AND LAUNCH STRATEGY

### 21.1 Confirmed Business Direction

Emerging Talks has confirmed that the initial launch will use a curated network.

The initial marketplace should be seeded with selected podcast hosts, podcasts and guests, with profile quality and relevance reviewed before wider access. The operational process, selection criteria, and initial inventory targets remain to be defined.

### 21.2 Decisions Still Open

The following decisions are intentionally unresolved and must be approved by the business owner before the development scope and commercial requirements are baselined:

- Monetisation model: subscriptions, paid services, per-booking fees or another model.
- Whether paid access is required at launch.
- MVP boundary: discovery and connection only, or the complete collaboration workflow through booking and interview tracking.
- Initial free and paid entitlements, if applicable.
- Payment service provider and supported payment methods.
- Launch inventory targets and curation/verification criteria.
- Initial success targets and launch-readiness thresholds.

Until these decisions are approved, pricing, plan limits, paid-feature entitlements, provider-specific payment requirements, and final MVP acceptance criteria shall remain provisional.

### 21.3 Recommended Decision Gate

Before development estimation and final sign-off, Emerging Talks should approve:

1. The launch user journeys and the minimum workflows required to validate marketplace demand.
2. The monetisation hypothesis to test, even if payment collection is deferred from the first release.
3. The initial curated host, podcast and guest acquisition plan.
4. The PSP selection criteria and provider decision.
5. The measurable launch targets and the owner responsible for each.

### 21.4 Curated Launch Operating Requirements

For the initial curated launch, the platform or its administrators should support:

- Inviting or approving selected hosts, podcasts and guests.
- Reviewing profile completeness and basic authenticity before publication.
- Recording the source and status of verification or curation decisions.
- Managing invitations, onboarding follow-up, and launch cohort status.
- Collecting feedback from the initial cohort and using it to refine matching and workflows.

The detailed curation workflow, review standards, and inventory targets require business-owner approval.
